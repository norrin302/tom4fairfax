About Tom

Three Decades of Results for Fairfax

Tom Peterson has served on the Fairfax City Council since 2022, bringing 42 years of professional experience and a BS in Biology from William & Mary, an MS in Environmental Management from Duke, and an MBA from the University of Texas at Austin.

He is a former White House Senior Advisor (Council of Economic Advisors & Council on Environmental Quality), the founder and CEO of the Center for Climate Strategies, and an adjunct professor at Johns Hopkins University and George Mason University.

As a Council Member, Tom has championed numerous environmental initiatives — protecting green spaces, advancing clean energy, and ensuring Fairfax remains a sustainable community for generations to come. He is a nonpartisan independent committed to practical solutions and measurable results.

Closing Statement

Building a Better Fairfax — Together

Fairfax is a special community with a unique character, strong neighborhoods, excellent schools, and engaged citizens. My focus has been on protecting those strengths while helping the City respond responsibly to rising costs, development pressures, infrastructure needs, public safety challenges, and long-term sustainability issues.

As your Council Member, I have worked to bring informed, independent, community-focused leadership to City government — focused not on politics, but on practical solutions and measurable results.

Together, we can continue building a Fairfax that remains financially strong, environmentally responsible, safe, welcoming, and true to the values that make our City unique.
