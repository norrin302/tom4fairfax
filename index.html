About Tom

Proven Leadership
for Fairfax

Since 2022, Tom has served on the Fairfax City Council, chairing the Environmental Sustainability Committee and working to make our city more sustainable, fiscally responsible, and livable for all residents.

A longtime Fairfax resident with decades of experience in public service and environmental policy, Tom brings fact-driven, independent leadership to every decision — focused not on politics, but on practical results.

The work of building a better Fairfax continues, and Tom is running for re-election to keep delivering — on fiscal stewardship, public safety, environmental protection, and the other issues that matter most to our community.

Fairfax is a special community with a unique character, strong neighborhoods, excellent schools, and engaged citizens. My focus has been on protecting those strengths while helping the City respond responsibly to rising costs, development pressures, infrastructure needs, public safety challenges, and long-term sustainability issues.
