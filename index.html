About Tom

A Leader Who Listens.
A Voice for All of Us.

Tom Peterson has served on the Fairfax City Council since 2022, representing residents across our city with a focus on responsible governance and community well-being. As a Council Member, Tom has championed numerous environmental initiatives — protecting green spaces, advancing clean energy, and ensuring Fairfax remains a sustainable community for generations to come.

A longtime Fairfax resident, Tom brings decades of experience in public service, environmental policy, and collaborative problem-solving to the Council dais. He believes in fact-driven, transparent governance — and in making sure every resident has a voice in the decisions that shape our community.

Tom is running for re-election because the work of building a better Fairfax continues. He's committed to smart growth, fiscal responsibility, and keeping our city moving forward — together.
